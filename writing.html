Escaping the Killer by Always Getting Closer

Can a path that gets closer to its target at every step still be infinitely long? Yes, on an Archimedean spiral. I derive the closed-form arc length, show L → ∞ as the radial step m → 0⁺, and connect the result to gradient descent: improving the loss at every step doesn't bound the length of the path.

Efficient Fibonacci computation: Édouard Lucas's Pascal-triangle method

Lucas's combinatorial formula gives the exact nth Fibonacci number without recursion, by summing a shallow diagonal of Pascal's triangle. I compare its cost with matrix exponentiation, Binet's formula and naive recursion, and show it suits resource-constrained environments.
